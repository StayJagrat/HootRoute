Streetwise

Walk like you know.

Maps pick the shortest path. Streetwise scores every block by what is open, lit and reachable at the minute you pass it, and shows its work.

Essence

Navigation for people who think

It does not tell you a street is safe or dangerous. It shows what is on it when you get there, and why the route it picked is better.

Show the work

Every score has a breakdown and a reason. No black box.

Say what is missing

Where data is thin, the score says so and shows confidence.

Streets, not neighborhoods

Conditions on a block. Never a label on a place or the people in it.

Stay calm

Precise and quiet. No alarms, no drama, no fear.

Logo

A lamp with a route through it

The amber disc is the lamp: what is alive on the street. The line is your walk, from where you start to the pin.

Wordmark

Bricolage Grotesque ExtraBold, tracking −1.5%. "Street" in Moon, "wise" in Lamp. Clear space: the height of the lamp disc. Minimum: 24 px mark, 96 px lockup.

Never

Recolour the lamp. Put the glow mark on a light background. Add a drop shadow. Stretch, rotate or outline the wordmark in another colour.

Colour

One brand colour, four signals

The product is a night product, so the brand is dark first. Amber is the only brand colour. Each signal has exactly one meaning, and colour never carries meaning alone: pair it with a number or a word.

Voice

Plain, specific, numbers first

Name the reason, not the feeling. Say when data is missing. Never use "safe", "unsafe", "dangerous" or "sketchy", and never name a neighborhood as a problem.

Motion

Lamplight

Motion is slow and warm, and it only ever shows something alive: a lamp, a route, an open place. Every animation has a still state that says the same thing.

The crew

Agents speak in first person, briefly, and say what they did

Scout

Plans and re-scores routes. "Streetwise route scores 69 against 55, 1.1 minutes longer."

Place agents

Shops declare live hours. "Peet's Coffee will stay open until 1 AM."

Watcher

Follows the walk. "Stopped near an open shop. Staying quiet."

Rescuer

Runs the alert ladder. "Guardian link opened. No call to 911."

Reporter

Files what the walk found. "Draft 311 report saved. Nothing submitted."

Night Auditor

Finds stale data overnight. "491 places have no listed hours."
