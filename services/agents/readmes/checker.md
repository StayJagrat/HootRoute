# HootRoute Checker

Before the walk: finds places on the route whose opening hours are unknown and checks their public listings live (Apify), so the Guide can re-score with evidence. During the walk: notices a stop on a stretch with nothing open and checks in. A listing is not proof a place is staffed. Talk to the HootRoute Head in ASI:One.
