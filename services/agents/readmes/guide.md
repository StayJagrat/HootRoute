# HootRoute Guide

Plans and scores walking routes in San Francisco with a deterministic engine: listed-open places at the minute you pass them, gaps between them, help points, mapped lighting, main roads, transit running, turns. Re-plans when you leave the route or a shop updates its hours. No crime data, no model in the score. Talk to the HootRoute Head in ASI:One.
