# Streetwise Night Auditor

On an interval, asks the Streetwise engine how complete its data is: how many places have no listed hours (they count as closed), how many street-lamp locations were inferred from past 311 reports, and how many open outage reports are subtracted from lighting. Saves `audit_latest.json`. Ask Scout for `audit`.
